campfire.ogg <- "fireplace sound loop" by pagdev, OpenGameArt (https://opengameart.org/content/fireplace-sound-loop), CC0
  fire.wav (29 s, stereo 32-bit) -> mono, high-pass 200 Hz x2 (removes low rumble that buzzed on small speakers),
  low-pass 9 kHz, light compression on the loudest pops, mean about -28 dBFS / peak -3 dBFS, OGG Vorbis q5.
  Filtered over three back-to-back copies and the middle one kept, so the loop seam stays clean.
  Without this file the game falls back to the synthesized fire in audio/FireSynth.java.
